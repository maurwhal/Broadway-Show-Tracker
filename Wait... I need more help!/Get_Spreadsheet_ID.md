# How to Get Your Spreadsheet ID

1. Open your Google Sheet in your browser

2. Look at the URL in the address bar:

   `docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ABC123xyz456DEF789/edit`
   
   (Your ID is the long string between `/d/` and `/edit`)

3. Copy everything between `/d/` and `/edit`

4. Paste it into the script where it says `YOUR_SPREADSHEET_ID`

That's it!
